Privacy policy

This OAuth client is used solely by its owner, with the open-source tool rclone running on the owner's own computer, to access the owner's own Google Drive.

  • No data is collected, stored, or processed on any server operated by this app. There is no server.
  • Google Drive data is transferred only between Google and the owner's own computer.
  • No data is shared with, sold to, or disclosed to any third party.
  • Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page.
